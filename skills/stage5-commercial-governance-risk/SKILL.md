# Stage 5: Commercial Governance & Risk Skill

This skill guides the commercial and contractual framing of pre-sales engineering proposals, protecting project profitability through defensible engagement models, strict Change Request (CR) controls, client homologation SLAs, and the non-negotiable **Tacit Acceptance Clause (Aceite Tácito)**.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Defensive Commercial Architecture**:
   - Technical estimation is useless without contractual safeguards.
   - If requirements are exploratory or legacy systems are unverified, reject turnkey Fixed Price contracts or mandate a Paid Inception Phase.
2. **Change Request (CR) Protection**:
   - Scope creep happens when minor changes are absorbed without formal cost/schedule impact analysis.
   - Enforce an explicit threshold: any deviation > 10% of sprint capacity triggers a formal CR with client written sign-off.
3. **Tacit Acceptance SLA**:
   - Prevent endless homologation delays where clients freeze billing by simply not testing the release.
   - Mandate a **5-business-day client UAT window**. If no Severity 1 (Blocker) bugs are logged, the milestone is **tacitly accepted**.

---

## 2. Execution Runbook

1. **Ingest Handoffs 1 to 4**: Review Scope, Architecture, Effort, and Staffing Plan.
2. **Select Commercial Model**: Compare Dedicated Squad, T&M, Fixed Scope, and Staged 2-Contract.
3. **Draft Change Request Protocol**: Detail submission, impact analysis, approval SLA, and schedule adjustment.
4. **Define UAT Terms & Tacit Acceptance**: Insert the contractual legal clause for 5-day tacit homologation.
5. **Assemble Pricing Support Memory**: Formulate internal guidance on total project hours, margin buffer, and milestone billing splits.
6. **Emit Handoff 5**: Pass `Handoff 5: Commercial Strategy` to the Lead Orchestrator for synthesis into `DOSSIE_TECNICO_PRE_PROPOSTA.md`.
